About the Project

The Car Service Management System is a full-stack web application developed to simplify and digitize vehicle service operations. It allows users to register, add their vehicle details, and request servicing online without manual intervention. The system provides real-time tracking of service requests, improving transparency between customers and service providers. Administrators can efficiently manage customer data, monitor service requests, and update service status. Overall, the application enhances service efficiency, reduces paperwork, and ensures a smooth user experience.
